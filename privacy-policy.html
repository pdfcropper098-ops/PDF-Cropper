Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Your Privacy Matters

PDFCropper is committed to protecting your privacy. We process all PDFs locally on your device and never upload your documents to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Email address when you contact us for support
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Usage Analytics: App usage statistics to improve our service (anonymized)

Document Processing

PDFCropper processes PDF documents entirely on your device. We do not:

  • Upload your PDF files to our servers
  • Store copies of your documents
  • Access the content of your PDFs
  • Share your documents with third parties

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To provide and maintain our PDF cropping service
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide technical support
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

All PDF processing happens locally on your device. Your documents never leave your device during the cropping process.

Security Measures

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit
  • Secure storage of any collected analytics data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal information

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics for app usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Crash Reporting: To identify and fix app crashes
  • App Distribution: Google Play Store for app distribution

These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request transfer of your information
  • Objection: Object to processing of your information

6. Children's Privacy

PDFCropper is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@pdfcropper.com
  • Contact Form: Contact Page
